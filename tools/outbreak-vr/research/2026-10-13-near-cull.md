# File #1 near-camera cull: why things vanish up close, and the patch (2026-10-13)

SLPM-65428 (English patch), Kevin, local slot 0, offline. Static work on a 32 MB RAM dump taken in J's Bar with
the GAME overlay resident (u32 `0x540004` = 3), then live, headset-free checks on the portable dev install
(`D:\dev\PenguinScreen2-build\run-dev`, no OpenXR runtime, `PCSX2_VR_TEST_INPUT=1` with a scripted PINE head,
network adapter off, never faster than normal speed). No C++ changed: the patch is in the File #1 profile.
Dumps, captures and scratch scripts stay in the session scratchpad; the repeatable test is
`tools/outbreak-vr/nearcull_test.py`.

## Short answer

- **One routine culls everything near the camera**: `FUN_005902F0` (GAME overlay). It answers "culled" when the
  model's feet + 100 units up are nearer the eye than the radius for the model's class. Seven call sites, all draw
  passes; a culled model's draw call is skipped.
- **The camera's focus character is never tested.** The game sets the focus to the local slot. The old profile hid
  your body by pointing the focus at an empty slot, so the cull caught you, and lowered the class-1 radius from 80 to
  75. Anything else nearer than 75 vanished too: a zombie at about 50 cm across the floor from the eye.
- **The patch** (profile only, draw-only, restored on disarm): the cull's first branch NOP'd so it always takes its own
  "not culled" exit. Then three branches that draw the focus character are turned to skip it (its body, its first
  pass and what it holds). The focus is left to the game, plus one repair entry for savestates made under the old
  focus hold.
- **Measured** (slot 5, a zombie walks up; about 50 samples a second):
  - **Before:** the zombie was not drawn whenever it was nearer than 75.0 by the cull's measure.
  - **After:** it was drawn in every sample, down to 65.0 (35 cm across the floor). The body stayed hidden, and the
    partners were drawn as before.
- **Near plane:** the game's projection uses near = 1 unit (1 cm), so the camera clips nothing a person brings near.

## The cull routine

`FUN_005902F0(a0 = &position, a1 = class)`, in the GAME overlay:

```
5902F0  addiu sp,sp,-48
590300  lb    v0, 0x48BF88          ; a global the game sets in some modes
590304  beq   v0, zero, 0x590314    ; 0x10400003   <- the patched word
590308  daddu s0, a1, zero
59030C  beq   zero, zero, 0x590388  ; return 0: not culled
590310  daddu v0, zero, zero
590314  p = position + (0, 100, 0)  ; f32 0x42C80000 = 100.0 added to y
59034C  jal   0x1916E0              ; f0 = |p - eye|, eye = f32 x3 at 0x306338
590358  radius = f32 [0x6D6CF0 + class*4]
590370  c.lt.s f0, radius ; bc1t ; a0 = 1 (culled) else 0
```

**Radii** (f32 table `0x6D6CF0`, GAME overlay data): class 0 = 20, class 1 = 80 (people, zombies), class 2 = 180,
class 3 = 300. An enemy's class is the s8 at `+0x0C` of the 16-byte row for its variant (`+0x54D`). The row is in the
table that the u32 at `0x357500 + type*8` points to (type = record `+0x03`). The only reader of the radius table is
`0x590360`. Nothing in the game writes it.

**The early exit:** while u8 `0x48BF88` is set, the routine never culls. That byte is read in about 40 places, so we
leave it alone and NOP the branch instead.

### Callers (all of them; `jal 0x5902F0` scan of the whole dump)

| `0x345114`, `0x345138` | characters' first pass, `0x345020` (records and the NPC list); calls `0x680590`, not identified (maybe the shadow) | records, NPCs | 1 | yes | `0x48AEA0`, `0x48AEA1`, `0x48AEA3` |
| `0x3451E0` | enemies' first pass, `0x345170` | enemy list (gp-0x5E7C, next +0x1C) | per type | n/a | `0x48AEA2` |
| `0x688060` | enemies' main draw, `0x688010` | enemy list | per type | n/a | `0x48AEA2` |
| `0x65174C` | NPCs' main draw, `0x651720` | NPC list (gp-0x5E6C) | 1 | n/a | `0x48AEA3` |
| `0x560C80` | held and attached objects, `0x560B60` (object list gp-0x5EB8, flag `0x20000` at +0x23C; the owner is the item record's owner byte - 1) | objects | 1 (the owner's position) | yes (owner == focus) | the object's own +0x01 |

**What happens when culled:** the draw call (`jalr` through the record's `+0x18`) is skipped. The main passes clear
the record's u8 `+0x198` first and set it to 1 only after the draw, so `+0x198` is "drawn this frame".

**Who else reads `+0x198`:** `0x5B9EF4` (a callback, skips its work for an undrawn character) and `0x620904` (a list
gp-0x5E98 whose `+0x2C` points at a character). Both look like effects tied to a visible character. **Assumption:**
neither is game logic that matters online. No other loads or stores of `+0x198` exist in the code.

### The focus slot and the hide flags

**The focus slot**, u8 `0x3AEF74`:
- written only by the room camera's init, `0x58DED8`, from the local slot byte `0x48BF7D`;
- the draw passes compare a record's `+0x03` with it.

**The hide flags**, u8 `0x48AEA0`-`0x48AEA3` (the focus character, the other characters, the enemies, the NPCs):
- **Written every frame** by the room camera update, `0x58EA88`-`0x58EAAC`:
  `AEA[k] = (cut[current].flags +0x1C4 >> k) & 1`.
  - The cut records are at `0x3AEF50 + k*0x198`; the current cut is u8 `0x3AEF55`.
  - They are also cleared at room setup (`0x5903A0`) and by the door follow camera (`0x5A94B0`).
- **So they are per-shot settings** of the fixed camera. A vsync hold cannot reach the draw.
- **Checked live** with a PINE write watch: the only writer during play was `0x58EAAC`, 25 hits a second.
- **A failed try:** a hold of `0x48AEA0` = 1 read back as 1 at every sample, yet the local record was drawn. A capture
  showed the inside of Kevin's head.
- **J's Bar:** all ten cuts have flags 0. Other rooms may hide characters per shot (see the open questions).

**The local slot byte, u8 `0x48BF7D`, is not stable within a frame offline.**
- `0x588C30` runs the AI partners' task lists with it set to 1 and then 2, and puts 0 back. It does this only while
  `0x1AF4D0()` says offline (u8 `0x3065C2` == 0).
- PINE polls saw 1 or 2 in about 0.3% of reads.
- So the patch reads nothing from it to pick a value. A first try held the focus "on the local slot" with four
  entries keyed on that byte. It could have handed the focus to a partner for a frame, and was dropped.
- **Open risk, not changed here:** `camera.base` resolves the local record from the same byte at vsync. The record's
  `+0x03` check passes for a partner's record too.

### The camera's near plane

- `0x345480` builds the projection: `jal 0x18F0E0` with far f32 `0x306378` = 50000 and near f32 `0x30637C` = 1.0.
  `0x345740` rebuilds it every frame from the FOV.
- With the eye at the head, nothing is nearer than 1 cm, so the near plane removes nothing a person or a zombie
  brings near.
- **Not checked:** GS- or VU1-side rejection of very large or partly behind-the-eye triangles. The game's VU1
  microcode was not read. In the captures a zombie 35-45 cm away drew whole.

## The patch (File #1 profile, `camera.silence` and `camera.lookAt.hold`)

| Address | Game's word | While armed | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| `0x590304` | `0x10400003` (`beq v0,zero,+3`) | `0x00000000` | the cull always takes its "not culled" exit, for every class and every caller |
| `0x3452E0` | `0x1060000B` (`beq v1,zero,0x345310`) | `0x00000000` | characters' main draw: the focus character returns before its draw (the path the game takes when `0x48AEA0` is set) |
| `0x3450F8` | `0x10600014` (`beq v1,zero,0x34514C`) | `0x00000000` | characters' first pass: the same for the focus character |
| `0x560C74` | `0x10620008` (`beq v1,v0,0x560C98`: owner == focus, draw) | `0x10620030` (`beq v1,v0,0x560D38`: the next object) | the focus character's held and attached objects are skipped |

**Guards:** the two GAME-overlay words are guarded by `0x540004 == 3`. The two main-program words are guarded by their
own function's first word (`0x345210` and `0x345020` = `0x27BDFFE0`), which is always true. Why guard them at all:
- the CameraDriver forgets an entry whose guard fails while armed, which is right for overlay code that a reload
  replaces, but would leave main-program code patched;
- and only guarded entries get the one-shot repair after a savestate load.

**The repair hold:** `0x3AEF74` := 0 while it reads 3 and the local slot reads 0. Slot 3 of Alex's File #1 savestates
was saved under the old hold (focus 3), and the focus is only reset at the next room. Without the repair, the body
showed in that state; measured below. With no restore value it writes once and never puts 3 back.

**Removed:** the two focus holds (3 / 2 on an empty record) and the class-1 radius hold (75). The radius table is no
longer touched.

**Restore:**
- The CameraDriver puts each silenced word back on disarm (a grab, a door, a cutscene, a menu dropping first person,
  VR off), only if it still holds our value.
- An entry whose guard fails is left to the code reload.
- Measured: all four words read the game's own value one sample after the grab released first person.

**Online:** first person runs online only in private co-op. All four words only change which draw calls this console
makes. No character record, no hide flag and no game-logic state is written. So the patch is **draw-only**, and it is
the same in co-op, where every console decides its own draws. The repair hold writes the camera focus byte, a camera
global, never a record; it cannot pass online, where no savestate is loaded.

**Alternatives not taken:**
- **Radii to 0** (four f32 holds): this stops the cull just as well. But the old way of hiding the body depends on the
  cull, and the hide flags are rewritten every frame, so the focus draws still had to be skipped. One branch word is
  smaller.
- **Making the cull spare everyone but the local player** (code cave): it would need more code and a cave, and gain
  nothing over the above.

## Measurements (dev install, `nearcull_test.py`)

**The cull's measure** is the distance from the eye to the zombie's feet + 100 up. "Across the floor" is the
horizontal distance from the eye. "Drawn" is the zombie's `+0x198`.

### Slot 5: a zombie walks up and grabs Kevin

About 50 PINE samples a second, first person armed.

| | Before (HEAD profile) | After (this patch) |
|---|---|---|
| Samples with the zombie nearer than 80 by the cull's measure | 514 | 460 |
| ... of them not drawn | **356** | **0** |
| Nearest drawn | 75.0 (51 cm across the floor) | **65.0** (35 cm across the floor) |
| Farthest not drawn | 75.1 (exactly the held radius) | none |
| Local body drawn (`+0x198`) | 2 samples, the first two after arming (before the first frame) | the same 2 |
| Partners drawn | 1409 of 1410 | 1420 of 1422 |

- **The partners:** the missing samples are single reads landing mid-pass, between the clear and the set.
- **Repeat runs of the tool:** after, 446 near samples, 0 not drawn; before, 82 near samples, 25 not drawn, nearest
  drawn 75.2, farthest not drawn 75.0.
- **Captures:**
  - Before, facing the zombie 36 cm away: the frame shows the empty bar.
  - At 49 cm it still showed it. A capture trails its sample by a frame or two.
  - After, 45 cm away: the zombie is in the lower right of the frame, and no part of Kevin shows.
- **Speed:** slot 5 ran at 41-60% on the OSD in both variants. The dev install is GPU-bound there at 3x (GPU 100%),
  and the game counter rate was no lower with the patch (164.5/s before, 181.8/s after). Slot 6 ran at 100% in both.
  **Not judged:** the patch's own cost. More draws near the camera should be negligible.

### Slot 6: calm, looking down at the body (pitch -70), then behind

- The local record was never drawn while armed, before or after.
- The look-down captures are the same: floor and shadows only, no body and no gun.
- The partners walked about (AI running) and were drawn, as near as 1.55 m.

### Slot 3: saved under the old focus hold

| | Focus | Local `+0x198` |
|---|---|---|
| With the repair entry | 0 within 0.26 s of arming | 0 throughout |
| Without it (override variant) | stayed 3 | 1: the body showed |

### Restore

Slot 5's grab releases first person (`releaseWhen` stance 5). One sample later:
- `0x590304` = `0x10400003`, `0x3452E0` = `0x1060000B`, `0x3450F8` = `0x10600014`, `0x560C74` = `0x10620008`;
- the local record was drawn again in the game's camera.

## Verified, and assumed

**Verified:**
- **Static:** the routine, its callers and its comparison. The radius table and its single reader. The focus writer.
  The hide flags' per-frame writer (the static scan and a live write watch agree). The near-plane value.
- **Live:**
  - before and after on the zombie's drawn byte;
  - the body hidden (the drawn byte and captures);
  - the partners drawn;
  - the restore on disarm and the slot 3 repair;
  - the profile loads with no warning;
  - `vrtest.py --check-profile` passes (81 checks for File #1);
  - the VR self-tests pass, 21 of 21.

**Assumed, or not checked:**
- `0x680590`'s pass is the shadow: not identified.
- The `+0x198` readers `0x5B9EF4` and `0x620904` are effects.
- No VU1/GS rejection of near geometry beyond what the captures show.
- Behaviour in rooms whose camera shots set hide flags; J's Bar has none.
- Co-op: reviewed only, no co-op session run.
- A savestate saved while armed and loaded in flat mode: the patched words should be put back by the one-shot repair
  (all four entries are guarded). Reviewed, not run.

## What still needs the headset

- **Does a zombie at arm's length read right?** The near field (`stereo.firstPerson.nearest` 0.5 m) puts it at 0.5 m
  perceived at most, and nearer geometry flattens there.
- Does anything of Kevin show when you lean far forward or look straight down? His collar or hair could catch the eye.
  The draw is skipped for the whole model, so it should not.
- A grab: first person still hands over to the game camera. While grabbed, the game's camera stays at the head
  until its next shot, and the cull is back on, so the zombie can vanish there. That is the existing release
  behaviour, not this patch.

## Open questions

- **Per-shot hide flags in other rooms:** a cut with bit 1 set would hide partners in first person. The candidate
  patch, not made: `0x58EAA8` `sb a1, 0x290(v0)` (`0xA0450290`) to `sb zero` (`0xA0400290`), so a shot hides nothing
  while armed.
- **The local slot byte's mid-frame changes offline** and `camera.base` (above).
- **File #2 (SLPM-65692)** still uses the old focus and radius holds. Its addresses differ, and the scope is File #1.
