# Doors: why your own door drops out of first person (File #1, 2026-10-14)

ROADMAP road step 3. Static disassembly of the J's Bar RAM dump (`ram_now.bin`, session scratchpad, never committed) plus
one offline live trace on the `doors` build (run-doors, slot 4, scripted pad input, normal speed, no memory writes).
Labels: **verified (code)** = read from the disassembly; **live** = seen in the 2026-10-14 trace; **assumed** = not checked.

## Summary

- The drop-out is not the room load. It is two scripted camera shots the game starts **only for the local player**:
  the door-opening shot (room camera mode `0x3AEF75` = 1) when you trigger the door, and the arrival shot (mode 2) in
  the next room. First person's guard `0x3AEF75 == 0` fails in both, so it hands the camera back.
- The room load itself (scenario status `0x48BF60` = 3, fade, load, fade in) is one generic path for any exit that
  takes the local player out of the room. It cannot be skipped and should not be.
- Proposed patch: NOP the camera-mode calls (`jal 0x590B40`, word `0x0C1642D0`) at the door-shot sites. Every other
  door side effect stays: the motion, the timer, the door-opened bits, the network notify, the room change request.
  Not applied yet (see "Not done").

## How a door works (verified, code)

1. **Trigger.** Pushing into a door for 1 s (0.5 s when `u8 0x30659C` != 0) runs the door search at the end of the
   action handler `FUN_0061D1F0`: wall probe class 2, local player (`0x1AF540`), `+0xBA0 & 0x90` clear, `+0xF80 & 1`,
   facing the face normal; frame counter `+0xCDE`; then `FUN_00631760(rec, kind 1, mode 5)` at `0x61D744`. Circle goes
   through the pickup handler `FUN_0061DB40` (call at `0x61DC28`). Both then call `FUN_0061D7A0(rec, 0)`.
2. **The door node callback `FUN_00591160`** (s1 = trigger node, s0 = the character at node `+0x34`) fills the
   character's pending door: `+0xAB8` (pending), `+0xAB9` (door kind), `+0xABA` (1 = leads out of the room). For the
   local player only (`0x1AF540(rec)` == 1) it also calls `0x590B40(1)` (camera mode 1, the door shot) and
   `0x5400A0(2, 40, 60)`, and ORs the door's bit into the per-room door table `u16 0x48AFB2 + room * 12`
   (`room` = `u16 0x3065AC`). Six such sites in `FUN_00591160`: `0x5914FC 0x5916F0 0x591804 0x5918C0 0x5919F8 0x591B0C`;
   six more in the sibling `FUN_005929E0`: `0x592D78 0x592F34 0x59302C 0x5930E8 0x5931FC 0x5932EC`. All are
   `jal 0x590B40` with `addiu a0, zero, 1` in the delay slot.
3. **Start of the motion `FUN_0061D7A0(rec, a1)`.** Stance 1, sub-action `+0x09` = u16 table `0x6DD9B0 + kind * 4 +
   a1 * 2` (two entries per kind: [0] opening, [1] arriving), then `0x635920` (set action). Kinds 1 and 2 with a1 = 0
   use 23 (24 when `+0xABA` == 0). Pairs seen: kind 1/2 (23, 95), 7 (42, 44), 8 (43, 45), 15 (68, 69), 16 (91, 92),
   20 (66, 67), 23 (101, 67), 26 (103, 104). If a1 = 0, stance != 1 and the record is the local player, it calls
   `FUN_00637B90` at once (leave the room with no motion).
4. **The motion handlers** (stance-1 sub-action table `0x6DED10`, indexed by `+0x09`; e.g. 23 = `0x63CAD0`,
   42 = `0x63F060`, 43 = `0x63F5D0`, 66/101 = `0x63F820`, 68/91 = `0x63FC40`, 103 = `0x645C10`) step through `+0x0A` /
   `+0x0B`. When `+0xABA` != 0 and the record is human (`+0x02` == 0) they call `0x1AF240(slot)` (a network notify:
   `0x821B10` offline / `0x8212F0` mode 1, nothing in modes 2/3) and, after a per-kind timer (`+0x0C` against
   f32 `0x6E78FC + kind * 16`), `FUN_00637B90(rec)`.
5. **`FUN_00637B90(rec)`** sets `+0xBA0 |= 0x10000` (left the room) and, only when `0x1AF540(rec)` says it is the local
   player, `u8 0x3065A8 = 1` (room change request). It is the only non-zero writer of `0x3065A8`.
6. **Scenario status 2 handler `0x5CDB90`** sees `0x3065A8` and calls `0x5CE710(3)` (status 3). The status-3 handler
   `0x5CE080` runs the load in sub-steps `0x48BF61` 0..5 (fade out 32 frames, load, fade in 120) and returns to status 2,
   clearing `0x3065A8`. The status table is `0x70A700`.
7. **Arrival.** In the new room `FUN_006391E0` calls `FUN_0061D7A0(rec, 1)` (arriving motion, e.g. 95), and
   `FUN_00550220` sets the arrival door kind and calls `0x590B40(2)` (the arrival shot) at `0x550494 0x5504E0 0x55052C`.

Camera mode setter: `0x590B40` (`sb a0, 0x3AEF75; sb zero, 0x3AEF52`).

## The follower path (assumed, not traced)

Every partner door goes through the same callback and motions, but the camera calls in step 2 and step 7 are behind
"is the local player", so a partner's door never moves your camera. When you follow a partner, you most likely cross a
door that is already open; which branch of `FUN_00591160` that takes (the door table `0x4747C1 + id * 0x60`, node
`+0x6C`) was not traced. Sending your own door down "the follower path" is not needed for the camera: the difference
that matters is the two camera-mode calls, and they can be removed directly.

## Proposed patch (not applied)

Camera silence entries, each `on: 0x00000000, off: 0x0C1642D0`, `when: [{ address: 0x540004, equals: 3 }]`,
`offlineOnly: true`, behind a new VR switch (for example `[VR] SeamlessDoors`, off by default):

- the 12 door-shot sites in `FUN_00591160` / `FUN_005929E0` listed above. NOP-ing the `jal` leaves the delay slot
  (`addiu a0, zero, 1`) harmless; the `0x5400A0` call and the door-table bit right after still run.
- The arrival shot (`0x550494 0x5504E0 0x55052C`) cannot be done with camera silence as it is: silence words are put
  back when first person disarms, and it disarms for the whole load (status 3), so they would be restored before
  `FUN_00550220` runs. It needs a patch kept through the load (an entry flag "keep while the switch is on", still
  restored on a network-mode change), or first person re-arming as soon as status is 2 with mode 2 tolerated.

Expected result: you see your character's own door-opening motion in first person for about 1.7 s, then the black
fade and load (first person is off only for the load), then you arrive.

Risks:
- **Unverified:** whether the door motions or the room camera wait on camera mode 1/2 finishing (a stuck door, or the
  arrival camera never handing back). The trace shows the motion ends on its own timer, which suggests not.
- `0x5400A0(2, 40, 60)` right after the call was not identified (sound, rumble or fade).
- In first person the eye is at the head while the body walks through the door frame: near-cull and body hiding apply,
  but the door model may clip through the view.
- Online: the camera calls are local only, but the patch is still `offlineOnly` until a private co-op session checks
  that nothing else depends on the shot (the notify `0x1AF240` is untouched).

## Live check of the NOPs (offline, PINE writes, game side only)

Second run, same slot and script, with all 15 `jal 0x590B40` words (the 12 door-shot sites and the 3 arrival sites)
written to 0 after the load, each checked to read `0x0C1642D0` first:

- camera mode `0x3AEF75` stayed **0 for the whole door**: no door shot, no arrival shot;
- the door ran exactly as before: motion 23 at 0.11 s, `+0xBA0` 0x10000 at 0.34 s, status 3 at 1.72 s (1.76 s
  unpatched), room 1 to 4, arriving motion 95 at 10.75 s, status 2 at 11.71 s, motion 0 at 13.27 s;
- partners kept their own motions; nothing stuck.

So the door motions and the load do not wait on the shots. Not checked: the picture (no capture taken), that first
person actually stays armed through the opening motion (this headless run had no head camera: the eye never sat at the
head, with or without the NOPs), and the door's sound/rumble (`0x5400A0`).

## Implemented (branch `doors`)

- `[VR] SeamlessDoors` (bool, off by default; Config.h, Pcsx2Config.cpp, STATUS switch table).
- `camera.silence` gained two keys: `whenSetting: <switch>` (only `SeamlessDoors` is known; an unknown name drops
  the entry) gates an entry on the switch and on the game being strictly offline (no network adapter at all, so not
  private co-op, and not online-safe); `keepThroughDisarm: true` (needs `whenSetting` and `when`) hands the entry to a
  pass the camera driver runs every vsync the VM runs, armed or not, put back when the switch goes off, the adapter
  comes on, first person (VR + HeadCamera) is off, or `when` fails; reset when the VM goes away; a savestate load
  repairs a kept NOP saved in the state while the gate is closed.
- File #1 profile: the 12 door-shot sites as ordinary gated entries (patched only while armed, put back on disarm),
  the 3 arrival sites with `keepThroughDisarm` (the arrival shot runs during the load, while first person is off). All
  `on: 0, off: 0x0C1642D0`, GAME overlay guard, `offlineOnly`. Words are only ever written over their own value.
- Self-test "Seamless doors" in vr_selftests (the shipped profile parses 15 gated entries, 3 kept, the rest ungated).

The push path ran exactly as in the code: wall class 2, face normal (-1, 0, 0), `+0xF80` bit 1 set, the counter rose 1 a frame to 29, then `FUN_00631760(rec, 1, 5)` / `FUN_0061D7A0(rec, 0)` fired at 1.0 s. | 2 | (9250, 12956), 89.4 deg | Walked on freely to x 9294, where the doorway's walk-in trigger fired at 0.69 s: kind **31**, leads out 1, motion 42, `+0xBA0` 0x10000. Room 1 to 4 at 1.87 s. The counter never rose (no wall in front). |

So holding the stick into the door works in first person. Run 1 stood about 9 units (9 cm) toward -z and 3 deg off: the
character was against the door's jamb, not in the doorway. The game's own push then picked another trigger there (kind
24, which does not lead out; what it is was not identified). Nothing of first person was in the way:
- the heading held at 86.7 deg through the push (body follow only runs when idle; no aim);
- the counter was never reset;
- camera mode stayed 0.

Circle uses the pickup handler's own search (`FUN_0061DB40`), which found the real door (kind 1) from the jamb too.

Why it may still bite in the headset: you walk where you look and cannot see your shoulders, so a little head yaw puts
you against the jamb. Possible later help (not done): a slight steer toward the doorway's centre while the stick pushes
into a door, or calling the Circle search on a stick push. Neither is needed for the door to work.
