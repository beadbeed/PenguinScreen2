# VR body: the partner shown as the game's own character, posed by their real head and hands (2026-10-21)

Branch `vrbody` (from `vrpresence`). `[VR] VrBody` and `[VR] VrBodySelf`, off by default. Evidence words as elsewhere:
**written**, **reviewed**, **built**, **self-tests passed**, **checked offline loopback** (one headset-free emulator and
`presence_echo` standing in for the partner). Nothing here is checked on a headset or between two machines.

Alex (2026-10-21): "I want the Resident Evil Outbreak 3D models, rigged and ready for VR ... the actual thing." So no
stand-in avatar: the game's own Kevin (or whoever the partner plays) with its own skeleton, its head and arms posed by
the partner's VR head and hands, its legs, hips and walking the game's own animation. The VR presence placeholder (a
grey head shell and teal hands in an impostor) is gone; the pose channel stays.

## 1. The skeleton (verified statically on `truevr/st04`, `melee/ram5_a`, `ts/ss7` and 19 more dumps; File #1)

Records `0x476DD0 + k * 0x10E0`; model `*(record + 0x34)`; u16 bone count at model `+0x816`; per bone a pointer at
model `+0x820 + 4 * bone` to a 0x190-byte struct:

| offset | what |
|---|---|
| +0x00 | world matrix, 4 x 4 row vectors, row 3 the joint; carries the character's uniform scale (Kevin 1.05, partner 1 1.10, the woman 1.01: record `+0xB4`) |
| +0x40 | local matrix (relative to the parent; unit axes); world = local x parent world |
| +0x80 | inverse bind (identity axes: the bind pose is a T-pose with every bone frame along the model's axes) |
| +0xC4 | s16 bone index; +0xC6 u16 channel tag |
| +0xC8 / +0xCC / +0xD0 | parent / child / sibling struct pointers |
| +0x104.. | the animation's working Euler XYZ, scale and translation |

Every player character seen (records 0-2 in all 22 dumps: 36, 36 and 43 bones) shares the first 36; parents from
`+0xC8` (exact):

```
 0 root   1 <- 0   2 pelvis <- 1 (105.5 up)   3 legs root <- 2   4-6 / 7-9 the legs (+X / -X)
10 waist <- 2   11 chest <- 10 (16 up)
12 clavicle <- 11 (+9.3, 16.8, -1.2)   13 shoulder joint <- 12 (8.3 along +X)   14 elbow <- 13 (26.1)   15 wrist <- 14 (25.1)
16 clavicle <- 11 (-9.3, ...)          17 shoulder joint <- 16 (-8.3)          18 elbow <- 17 (-26.1)  19 wrist <- 18 (-25.1)
20 neck <- 11 (20.7 up)   21 head <- 20 (7.4 up; 155 cm up in the world for Kevin)   22 <- 21, 23-27 face <- 22
32 hand <- 15 (offset 0), 33-35 thumb and fingers <- 32;   28 hand <- 19, 29-31 <- 28
woman only: 36-37 hair <- 21, 38-42 skirt <- 3
```

Rest lengths (Kevin, model units before the scale): upper arm 26.06, forearm 25.13; the woman 24.78 / 25.37. The bones
point along their parent's +-X in the bind pose; the face is at +Z (forward), +Y up. The model's +X side (12-15) is the
character's **left** (up x forward), the -X side (16-19) its **right**: the game puts the weapon on bone 19 (below).

## 2. When the game builds them (verified: memwatch on Kevin's and partner 1's bones, slot 6; code; the RE atlas)

- **Locals**: the animation writes every bone's local (`FUN_00193340` / `FUN_001912D0`, about 30 a second, from the
  Euler tracks) in the players' tick `0x638850`, which also runs the game's own head look (`FUN_0062F790`: bone 21's
  local turned toward what the character looks at, its subtree's worlds rebuilt).
- **Worlds**: once per tick in `FUN_00346810` (8 player records; called at `0x5CDEB0`), before `0x638850`: per record
  `FUN_005A6720` (bone 1's height, at `0x346884`), then `FUN_005A5BA0` / `FUN_00346B30` (the recursion
  `FUN_00192C00`, root parent `record + 0x50`), then the held items: `FUN_006313A0(record, item slot +0xC7C, bone 19)`
  and `(+0xC7D, bone 15)` copy the hand bone's world into the item object's matrix (`+0x1C0`, position `+0x200`).
- **Hit spheres** (`FUN_005AB840` reads bones 21 and 11), the hit test `0x5A9DB0` after.
- **Draw** later (`0x341B80` -> body pass `FUN_00345210` -> `jalr *(record + 0x18)` = `FUN_00341EB0` ->
  `FUN_003435F0`): it only skins (`FUN_001922B0`: inverse bind x world for each bone, into the VU1 buffer). Held items
  are drawn after the bodies by `FUN_00560B60` with their own matrices. The local character's body is skipped there in
  first person (the near-cull entries, `0x3452E0`).

So the held weapon is **not** drawn attached to the hand bone: it is put on it in the world build. A pose written at
the draw would leave the gun in the animated hand; a pose written as locals before the world build carries the gun, the
hit spheres and the hair with it.

## 3. The hook: caves U and V (written, built; self-tests interpret them)

- **Cave U** (the normal way). The site `0x346884` (`jal FUN_005A6720` in `FUN_00346810`, main program) calls cave U
  (`0xFD290`): U calls `FUN_005A6720` as before, then, for the slot of the host block naming this record (flag 8), writes
  its first `nloc` entries as those bones' **local** matrices: the neck 20 and head 21, and per driven arm the clavicle
  (its own animated local, so the arm hangs rigidly off the chest), shoulder, elbow and wrist. The game then builds the
  worlds, puts the held items in the posed hands and runs the hit tests on the posed body; the animation writes every
  local again right after (`0x638850`), so nothing stays and the host always reads the animation's own locals.
- **Cave V** at the draw. The site `0x345314` (`jalr *(record + 0x18)` in the body pass) calls cave V (`0xFD000`): for
  the slot naming the record, its remaining entries become world matrices = (the host's frame relative to the chest) x
  (the chest's world of this frame), through the game's own multiply `FUN_00190C50`. Used for the head's subtree (the
  game's head look rewrites bone 21 after the world build), for your own arms' "only the arms" (flag 2: every bone not
  kept gets zero axes, so its vertices fall onto its joint) and as the fallback (`PCSX2_VR_BODY_DRAWTIME=1`: all
  entries at the draw, flag 1 re-runs `FUN_006313A0` so the items follow, its first word checked first).
- **SELF** (`VrBodySelf`): the focus character's skip `0x3452E8` (`beq zero, zero, <return>`, `ld ra` in its delay
  slot) jumps to cave V's SELF (`0xFD228`): a slot with flag 4 naming the record is drawn through V; anything else takes
  the same return. Without the host's slot nothing changes from today's first person.
- **Free RAM**: the caves `0xFD000-0xFD374` (221 words, `camera.caves` registered: all or nothing with read-back, sites
  after the body, drained by the game counter), the matrix tables `0xFD400-0xFDFFF` (two of 0x600), the host block
  `0xFF300-0xFF3FF`. All read zero in the 22 File #1 dumps; the host refuses to write a block or tables that are not
  zero or ours. Caves of other agents: `0xFE000-0xFE8D8` (melee), `0xFE900-0xFEFFF` (vraim, handpickup); host blocks
  `0xFF000-0xFF2FF`.
- **The host block** (`0xFF300`): `"VBD1"`, a stamp, the attach function and its first word, two slots of 0x40 from
  `0xFF310` (0 the partner, 1 yourself): u32 record, u32 table, u8 count, u8 ref (the chest), u8 flags (1 attach, 2
  collapse, 4 self, 8 locals), u8 nloc, u32 keep mask x2, two attaches (u16 record offset, u8 bone), u8 bones[24], and
  cave U's stamp at +0x38. Written on the CPU thread at vsync (between EE slices), tables first, magic last; cleared when
  the switch goes off or nobody is posed.

Only bone matrices the game rebuilds every tick (and, in the fallback, the held item's matrix the game rebuilds every
tick too). Never a record, never the Euler parameters, never the game's traffic. In private co-op the partner's
character is animated locally on each console from the game's own messages (stance, position, heading, aim), so the
pose has to come over VR presence's channel, and writing it is local to each console.

## 4. The solve (`pcsx2/VR/VrBody.*`; written, self-tests passed)

Per character every vsync, in the chest's frame (the chest's world built from `record + 0x50` down the chain of
locals, so a matrix the cave wrote never feeds back; the character's scale divided out):

- **Head**: the VR head's forward and up into yaw (about the chest's up), pitch and roll, clamped to 75 deg yaw, 55
  down / 45 up, 35 roll; the neck takes 40% of each, the head the full turn; the face (and the woman's hair) follow.
- **Arms**: the wrist target is the controller's grip moved 6 cm back along its forward. For the partner the hands are
  placed relative to their VR head at the character's eyes (head bone + (0, 7, 9)), so a partner who leans or whose
  recentre sits off the character still reaches from the right shoulders; your own hands (VrBodySelf) are where you see
  them. A target inside the chest is pushed out in front. Two-bone IK from the animated shoulder joint: the reach
  clamped to 0.999 of the arm and to the elbow's 150 deg bend; the elbow toward a pole down, out and a little back
  (a pole along the reach, or a target at the shoulder, falls back to any perpendicular). The upper arm's crease (+Z)
  toward the forearm's bend, the forearm on the same hinge. The hand: fingers (+-X) along the controller's forward, the
  thumb (+Z) along its up; the wrist's twist split half to the forearm, the rest clamped to 90 deg and the swing to 75.
- **Blend**: each part eases in and out over 0.25 s; an arm goes back to the game's animation when its hand is not
  tracked, its target is past 1.3 arm lengths from the shoulder or more than 25 cm behind the chest.
- **Which hand is which**: the game's handedness through this console's own mapping (a VR point to the left against up
  x forward in the game): the VR left hand drives the model's +X arm in File #1.
- Legs, hips, the walk and the turn stay the game's.

## 5. Self-tests (`VR body`, `VR body (File #1 profile ...)` in `tests/vr/VRSelfTests.cpp`)

Frames (compose and inverse, the game's matrices and their scale, NaN and skew refused, `Mul44` in the game's order);
IK (reach, out of reach, the bend limit, a target at the shoulder, a pole along the reach, no pole, a zero bone or a
NaN); head angles; the bone map against Kevin's and the woman's parent tables (and broken ones), subtrees; the whole
solve on Kevin's bind pose (both wrists on target to 1e-6, the hands along the controllers, the elbow below the
shoulder, the wrist's clamp, the torso push, a half weight, the head clamped at 75 with the neck at 30, nothing
tracked); the weights and their easing; the block layout. Then caves U and V and SELF from the profile's own words in
the test's EE interpreter: U's locals (only nloc of them, a bone past the model's count skipped, another record left
alone), V's world entries after the locals, the fallback's world, attach and draw order, the attach skipped when its
first word is not the game's, the self collapse (axes zeroed, joints kept, kept bones untouched), SELF's two returns,
and no magic: the game's calls only.
