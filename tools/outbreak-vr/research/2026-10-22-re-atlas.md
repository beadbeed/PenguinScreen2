# Resident Evil Outbreak File #1: an RE atlas for one-to-one VR (2026-10-22)

Alex, 2026-10-21: "I want this to really feel like a one-to-one experience... Resident Evil Outbreak VR, not something
thrown over the top." This note does three things:
1. It audits every VR feature in the build: the game's own, or ours on top.
2. It maps the game systems we have not touched yet, as far as this pass got.
3. It ranks the eight reverse-engineering conversions that would move the build the most.

How this was made: read-only and static. It uses the J's Bar RAM dump (`ui/ram_now.bin`) and the savestates already
extracted to the session scratchpad (`truevr/f1_0N`), never committed. No emulator, no builds and no game writes. It
builds on every note in this folder (`2026-10-21-true-stereo.md` is on branch `truestereo`, `-vr-presence.md` on
`vrpresence`, `-hand-pickup.md` on `handpickup`, `-grab-first-person.md` on `grabdoor`) and on the 2026-10-15 review.

Labels:
- **verified (code)**: read in the disassembly;
- **verified (memory)**: read in a dump;
- **assumed**: reasoned, not checked;
- **open**: not mapped yet.

**Coverage:** the five parallel static surveys (sound, animation, HUD and event scripts, AI senses, collision) are folded
into §2. §2 follows their final reports (`scratchpad/reatlas/<area>/REPORT-final.md`, written after the first hand-back),
which are authoritative. My own verified findings from reading their scratch disassembly stay where they do not
conflict; where they do, the last paragraph of each subsection says which source I used and why.

## 1. The "on top" audit

**Labels used in this section:**
- **Game:** the game's own code or assets do it (we may patch a word or drive an input).
- **Hybrid:** the game's code decides the outcome, but our approximation feeds it.
- **On top:** our own drawing, input mapping or estimate.

walk-and-shoot, which is deliberate. The other 10 are what reads as "thrown over the top":
1. the mono picture on a screen;
2. the CC0 hands and the impostor weapons, drawn by us even though the meshes are the game's;
3. the laser, the muzzle flash and the recoil;
4. the haptic guesses;
5. the wrist card;
6. the toasts and the pickup cue;
7. the zone markers;

The constant eye height hides inside the head-camera hybrid. Every one of these now has a game-side replacement named,
mapped (the shot, the head bone, the hand bone, the near cull, the prompt) or with its next step written down (sound,
the HUD text pass, scripts, collision).

### (a) Sound: positioning and rumble mapped; the IOP side open

Sources: `sound/REPORT-final.md` (authoritative) and my own reading of the same routines. Where they differ, the last
paragraph of this subsection says which one is used and why.

**The listener is the game camera (verified (code)).** Every positional sound goes through one routine that reads the
view matrix at `0x3062E0`. First person writes that camera from the head, so sound follows the head wherever the head
pose reaches `0x3062E0` before the call (assumed: up to one tick late).

| Address | What | Label |

**Rumble (verified (code), from the sound report).**
- Path: `0x1B9060(pattern)` -> `0x1B8E00(port 0)`, gated by the option byte `0x3065BF` and the capability word
  `0x2F87D0`; send `0x1B8F10` -> `0x189388`. The pad must be actuator-capable (`0x292D60+0` == 1) or `0x2F87D0` stays 0.
- Player at `0x2F87D0`: `+1` active, `+0xC` countdown, `+0xE` step, `+0x10` pattern pointer.
- Patterns `0x217220[9]`: 1 a 15-frame small motor; 2 large 0xBF for 7 frames then small 8; 3 large 0xFF 7 then small 8;
  4 large FF, BF, 7F 8 frames each plus small 6; 5 DF, small, 9F 10 frames each; 6 7F 5 plus small 5; 7 BF 10; 8 FF 15.
- Main event hook: `0x634260(record, kind)`, local only. Kind 3 is damage (by `+0x138`: <= 200 pattern 1, < 700 pattern
  2, else 3), kind 4 pattern 4, kind 5 firing (per weapon via `0x5C1240` / `0x6E5948` -> `0x6DEC68`, from `0x62CFA4`),
  kinds 6, 7 and 8 patterns 1, 2 and 3. `0x636990` pattern 8; `0x575550` pattern 1 plus a camera shake
  (`0x58EC30`); `0x5BEDD0` and `0x5BF200` are script vibration.
- No heartbeat or Danger rumble exists on this path, so §1's "the game's own Danger event" has no rumble to forward.

**VR spatial audio, recommended (sound report).** Hook `0x1B7C28` (the `jal 0x191A10` in `0x1B7B50`) with a cave that
computes the view-space (xv, zv) from `0x3062E0` and writes sp+0xF0 = 322.5 + 320*sin(atan2(xv, -zv)) and sp+0xFC = +w,
so the pan covers 360 deg of azimuth. `0x1B7B88`'s eye lookup is where a real head position could replace the camera
eye. True per-voice spatialisation still needs a way to take a voice out of the SPU2 mix, which PCSX2 does not offer.

**Open:** whether the scratchpad `0x3F40` holds the view live (log sp+0xF0..0xFC once); the pan of ADX streams; the IOP
pan law (`0xE25EC`, `0xE29DC`); the SPU2 voice table (extracted, not analysed).

**Co-op (assumed):** all sound and rumble are local; partners' sounds are generated locally from their replicated
positions.

**Differences between my first reading and the final report.** Pan matrices: I read two scratchpad matrices
(`0x3700`, `0x3F40`) as inputs; the report says `0x3F40` is the view copied by `0x345740`. I use the report: it names
the copy. Footsteps: I guessed `0x1B7410` (group 3); the report names `0x1B7760`, and `0x1B7760` is the routine with the
2000 cm and room cull, so I use it. Rumble: I had "not found"; the report maps it. `0x5400A0` and the listener agree.

### (b) Animation: channels, tables, evaluation order and the head-look layer mapped

Sources: `anim/REPORT-final.md` (authoritative) and my own reading. Conflicts are listed at the end.

**Channels (verified (code)).**
- Blocks of 0x20 bytes: A at `rec+0x1E4 + 0x20k` (main skeleton), B at `rec+0x364 + 0x20k`; count `u8 rec+0x524` (player 5,
  zombie 2).
- Fields: `+0` f32 frame, `+4` end, `+8` loop start, `+0xC` loop byte, `+0xD` flags (0x08 blending, 0x10 paused, 0x20
  looped, 0x40 ended), `+0xE` u16 motion id (`rec+0x1F2` is channel 0, `+0x212` channel 1), `+0x10` blend step, `+0x14`
  blend t, `+0x18` blend frames left, `+0x1A` bit 0 frozen. Root motion delta at `rec+0x4E4` to `0x4EC`.
- Advance: `0x55C030(rec, step)` -> per channel `0x55BE60` -> `0x55BC80` (frame += step; returns 0 playing, 1 ended, 2
  looped) -> `0x192BD0`, which writes the pose into the local matrices. The step is 1.0, doubled when `u8 0x30659C`
  is non-zero (it reads 1; probably 30 Hz logic with 60 fps clips).

**Starting a motion (verified (code)).**
- There is no central animator. Action handlers are state machines on `rec+0x0A` (for example `0x54E990`): phase 0 sets the
  action `+0x54F` and starts a motion with `0x5A4880` or `0x5A4490(rec, id, startFrame, blend)` (538 call sites) ->

  id too. `0x5A4890` also clears channels 2 to 4 (`0x192500`) and writes pose offsets at `+0xCB8`, `+0xCC8`.. (not decoded).

(`0x1916E0`), `+4` u16 bearing (`0x346C50`, from the character's chest, bone 11, to the enemy's bone `+0x551`), `+6` u8 blocked (1 if
the distance is over 3000, else the wall ray `0x549860`), `+7` u8 a second ray (`0x54D4F0`, not decoded). NPCs against enemies fill
`0x3844F0 + 400*n + 8*j`. Per character, a u8 list of the enemy indexes in range (255 ends it) at `0x3840E0 + 50*i` (NPCs `0x383D50 +
50*n`), built with `0x6A4680` (assumed a distance sort). The 8x8 character matrix at `0x386110` is built only when `0x1AF4D0` returns 1.
The AI report says the writer of the line-of-sight table `0x385496` was not found: it is this routine. Readers: `0x5794A0` /
`0x579870` (distance: the cell, or live when the enemy's `+0xC94` bit 0x40 is set; `0x5794A0` masks the row `& 3`), `0x579660` /
`0x5799C0` (bearing; the stored `+0xBD8` when bit 0x80 is set), `0x5797A0` (blocked flag; 0 when bit 0x10 is set), and the player's
lock-on picker `0x62D3B0`/`0x62D700`.

**2. Senses `0x604870`, core `0x604D40` (verified (code)).**
- Parameters: sight range `+0xBCC` (table `0x6F6E80`; zombies up to 1800), rear range `+0xBD0` (700), cone `+0xC8C` (table `0x6F6E40`, 0x10000
  = 360 deg) relative to the zombie's own heading `+0x92`; patrons in J's Bar (memory): sight 400, 1800, 1e8; rear 100, 700, 200; cone 0x8000
  and 0x9FF6. Eye bone `+0xD55` (21 = head). Called as `0x604750(enemy, cone, eye bone, sight, rear)` from the think step.
- Awareness: u16 `+0xBF8[2][10]` (row 0 characters, row 1 NPCs), max 8191, above 4000 = target. Eligibility `0x604690`/`0x634640`: present,
  same room, status `+0xBA0` without bits 0x5E0, action `+0x54F` not 10, `+0xBA0` bit 8 clear, and not `0x636F60` (id `+0xB98` = 2 in action 0x41;
  assumed Jim playing dead).
- Hearing: within 800 cm, awareness += `0x6DC770[target +0xD10]` x a row byte (`0x71A08C + 0x60*(+0xB98)`). `+0xD10` is the noise class: idle 0, walk
  10, aim entry 1000, button action 3000. Gunshots do NOT raise noise; only the hit aggro `0x6057B0` does. Status bit 0x20000000 = unseen.
- Sight: skipped (hearing only) if the cache says blocked, if the distance exceeds the sight range, or for the unseen cases. Inside the
  cone: gain `0x605140` x1 (amount = 400 - 2d / divisor from `0x6FB4A0[+0xC89]`, minimum 1, times modifier bytes `+0xC20` to `+0xC23`, with a random
  term); outside the cone but within the rear range: x2; beyond: decay `0x6054B0` (2500 / `0x6FB4C0[+0xC8A]` + 1).
- Target pick `0x604000(enemy, 4000)`: results `+0x24` target, `+0xC25` kind, `+0xC26` index, `+0xBD4` distance, `+0xBD8` bearing, `+0xC94` bit
  0x800 found; mode `+0xBA4` (0 characters only `0x604220`, 1 characters then NPCs `0x604520`, 2 NPCs first); priority `+0xBB6[4]` from
  `0x6FB4E0`; a character within 100 cm of the table radius `0x6FB510[+3]` is picked at once (`0x604328`); height difference below 170;
  alert lock `+0xC94` bit 0x20. Offline crowd cap: 3 zombies per player (`0x6047B0`/`0x6047B4`).
- The AI reads the player's position, room, status, action, `+0xD10`, HP and `+0xB98`, and (my reading) the chest bone's world position for the
  cache rays and bearing. It does not read the heading.

**Aim class and the hit reaction (verified (code)).** `+0xBCD` is derived from `+0xCD4` + `+0xBC8` (§2b); `0x636E20` maps it to a class
only while the action is 0x80. Only type 19 uses it (`0x68E7B0`, inside `0x68E760`): a type-19 enemy in motion 102, 103 or 112 drops hits
whose class is 0 or 2. `0x68E760` otherwise applies `0x60F0C0` (staged damage `+0x138` to HP `+0x544`, 0x7FFF immortal; returns 0 alive, 1
dead, 2 heavy) and sets state 4 (dead) or 3 or 1 (hurt, heavy). The aim class from the real gun pitch (`0x646C98`) therefore matters only for
type 19.

**Grab (AI report).** The zombie attacks when the bearing is within cone/2 and the distance is under 100 (`0x666324`, `0x6664B4`) -> `0x677260`
(a non-master uses a follower table `0x6E57A0`). The victim's stance-5 handler is `0x60F340` (reactions table `0x6DD850`); `0x60F3F0` links
victim and zombie (`+0xB24`, `+0xB1D`, `+0xB1E`, `+0xB20`). The earlier observation stands: a grabbing zombie at 131 deg, Kevin facing 311
(`2026-10-21-grab-first-person.md`).

**For VR.** Head turns cannot spook zombies (no heading is read). To make gunshots alert zombies, set `+0xD10` on a shot. A VR body that

**Co-op.** Master test `0x60EF30`; awareness is synced (sender `0x606500`, receiver `0x609590`); VR changes to noise, cone or aim act only on

**Open:** the lock picker body `0x62D3B0`, the modifier bytes, `0x54D4F0`, `0x1AF4D0`, the type-19 and other attack code, and live behaviour.

### (f) Collision: world data, ray, floor, movement and dynamic faces mapped

Sources: `collision/REPORT-final.md` (authoritative) and my own reading.

**World data (verified (code, memory)).**
- Wall grid A header at `0x3938A8`: `+0` cell x, `+4` cell z (300 in J's Bar), `+8` nx, `+0xC` nz (6 x 6), `+0x10` origin x (7750), `+0x14` origin z
  (12200), `+0x18` cell array pointer (cell = `ix*nz + iz` -> a list of absolute face pointers ending 0xFFFFFFFF), `+0x1C` face base. Floor grid B has
  the same layout at `0x3938C8`. Global ignore mask `u32 0x3938E8` (0). Loaded by `0x5E4E00` at room load.
- Face, 0x38 bytes: `+0` flags (low byte class 0..15, high 16 bits attribute), vertices `+4`/`+0x10`/`+0x1C`, normal `+0x28`, plane d `+0x34`
  (n.p + d = 0). J's Bar: 143 wall faces (137 class 0, 6 class 1), vertical and one-sided, y -50 to 300 (some only 100 to 300, above doors);
  furniture is vertical box proxies with no tops. Grid B: 21 floor faces, mostly y = 0, a raised area at y = 20 (x 8100 to 8700, z 12450 to
  12886). Attribute words (flags >> 16) seen: 0x29, 0x2F, 0x12F, 0x22F.
- Dynamic faces: `0x70E750` (stride 0x38), count `s32 0x23BEC4`, cell list `0x70E6D0` (J's Bar 16, the bathroom 0; maximum about 36, assumed).
  The ray always includes this list.
- Attribute bit 0x1 blocks players (`rec+0x190` = 0x0001FFFF), bit 0x20 blocks enemies. Class 1 = door walls: slot `(attr >> 8) & 0xF` indexes the
  0x60-stride table at `0x4747C0`; the face is open when the byte at `+1` is 2 and attribute bit 0x40 is clear (assumed). In J's Bar the two
  class-1 attributes (0x12F, 0x22F) point at slots 1 and 2, whose bytes are 0, so those faces block.

**The queries (verified (code)).**
- `0x5494C0(a0 start, a1 end, a2 hit out, a3 normal out, t0 maskA, t1 maskB, t2 material out)` returns 1 on a hit: a pure grid ray cast, nearest
  hit, one-sided. 53 call sites. Walk `0x549610` (steps of about 170 through the cells), filter `0x5E6050` (masks laid out like the word
  `0x5E61E0` returns: class bits low, attribute bits high; maskB excludes; then the room-wide mask; class-1 faces go through the door rule),
  ray-triangle `0x5491E0`, point-in-triangle `0x5E58E0`. The any-hit form is `0x549860` -> `0x549930` (25 sites; the perception cache uses it).
  Callers pass (-1, 0) and treat attribute-word bit 0 plus bit 16 as solid. The wall probe `0x62DAE0` is a ray from the feet.
- Floor: `0x5E5B40(p, classOut, filter)` (it wraps `0x5E5C20`) returns the highest plane height of upward faces below p.y + 100; the record's floor
  y is `+0x170`, class `+0x174`, normal `+0x178`. A host replica matched 8 characters in 4 states.
- Movement: per tick `0x5CDE40` -> `0x6383E0` -> `0x5A9D20` (`0x5CDEA8`) -> per record `0x631CA0` -> `0x5A9AC0`: a sphere of radius `f32
  rec+0x1B8` (50.0) at the position plus (0, 10, 0) (enemies (0, 10, 15)), swept by `0x5E63E0`, sphere-triangle `0x5E6A90`, resolve `0x5E7280`; then
  a second sweep `0x54BF70` (not decoded). Measured: player centres never come closer than 50.0 to a blocking face. Enemies are handled by
  `0x606030` (list `gp-0x5E7C`; velocity `+0xB28` added directly when `+0x54A` bit 0x20 is set).
- Probes in action code (not decoded beyond): `0x62B880` (a ray back along the last displacement), `0x62ABA0` (four rays, nearest wall, heading
  difference), `0x629C30`, `0x629F70`, `0x62A8A0`, `0x62B180`, `0x62B380`, `0x62B4F0`.

**The game never collides its camera:** none of the collision functions is reachable from the room camera `0x58E7B0`, the rebuild `0x345740` or the mode
setter `0x590B40`.

**The head-in-wall fix (collision report, recommended: a read-only host replica).**
- Validate first: status `0x48BF60` == 2, room `u16 0x3065AC`, pointers in range, 1 <= nx, nz <= 32.
- Cache grid A's unique faces per room and the dynamic faces each frame; filter `attr & 0x1` plus the door rule.
- Segment test from the head point H0 (the character's head, from the skeleton) to the VR eye H1, exactly like `0x5491E0`; clamp the eye to the nearest
  hit minus 8 to 10 cm. Better: an 8 to 12 cm sphere around the eye; an optional fade past 15 to 20 cm; floor under the head from grid B
  (eye.y >= floorY + about 20).
- The alternative stays valid (my reading): a cave on the game thread calling `0x5494C0(&H0, &H1, &hit, &normal, -1, 0, &attr)`; it keeps no state
  beyond its stack.

**Co-op:** all local reads, view-only, nothing shared.

**Open:** `0x54BF70`; `0x54DB30`/`0x54D820` (called from `0x5AA9B0`; assumed attack against the world); the meaning of attribute bits beyond 0x1, 0x20
and 0x40.
