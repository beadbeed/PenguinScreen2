## 1. How the game does a grab

### Records

- Zombie: enemy table `0x3FFCA0 + 0xD90*n` (n < 64), update `+0x14` = `0x664290`. State `+0x08`, sub-state `+0x09`,
  phase `+0x0A`, target pointer `+0x24`, the code byte `+0xB1E`, the victim `+0xB24`. State table `0x6E49E0`: 1 awake,
  2 attack or hold (`0x66A950`, sub-states by `+0x09` through `0x6E4AB0`), 3 hurt. (code)
- Player: stance `+0x08` = 5 while grabbed. Stance 5's handler `0x563670` jumps by `+0x09` (table `0x709890`); the
  grab handlers through `0x6DD850` (sub 0 `0x60F3F0`, sub 1 `0x60FBA0`, ...). The grabbing zombie is `+0xB24`; the zombie's
  own `+0xB24` points back. (code; slot 5 live: Kevin `+0xB24` = `0x429530` = enemy 49)

### Start: range, angle, lunge, cooldown

- The approach sub-states (e.g. `0x666270`, `0x666400`) call the attack choice `0x677260` when the target's bearing from
  the zombie's own heading (`+0xBD8`) is within half its cone (`+0xC8C`; 40950 = +-112 deg on slot 5's zombie) and the
  distance `+0xBD4` is under 100 (game units, cm). `0x677260` runs the per-type choice (`0x6E57A0[+0x04]`; type 0
  `0x677320`) only as the room's master (`0x60EF30`). (code)
- `0x677320`: no attack while the zombie's awareness of the target (`+0xBF8[...]`) is under 4000; a wall check
  (`0x5E6200`); then within 150 units the grab (sub-state 6), otherwise `0x6780C0` (the swipe, sub-state 9: assumed).
  The player's facing does not matter; slot 5's zombie grabs from behind. (code; live)
- The lunge: zombie state 2 sub-state 6, motion 413 (82 frames). Kevin goes to stance 5 at its frame ~13: **0.23-0.27 s
  after the lunge starts** (live, three runs). The swipe is sub-state 9, motion 134 (Kevin to stance 3, about -100 HP).
- Cooldown: after an attack the zombie waits in state 1 sub-state 31 with `+0xE` counting 60 ticks (2.0 s) to 0, then
  attacks again. (live)

### The escape counter

- **The meter is the player's own `+0xCDD`** (s8): the low 7 bits are the count left, bit 7 the knife-counter flag.
  Set at the grab's phase 0 by `0x563AE0` from `0x6E7840[(sub == 1 ? 1 : 2) * 8 + rec[0xB98]]`: **30** for every
  character in sub-states 1 and 2 (no difficulty or condition term in that table or that code). (code; live: 30)
- Every tick in phases 4-6 `0x631060` takes off `0x630FC0`'s count (times 2 with bit 7): **1** for a direction pressed
  this tick (`+0xF78 & 0x0F`) and **2** for Cross / Circle / `0x200` pressed (`+0xF78 & 0x230`): at most 3 a tick. Never
  below 0; no regrowth (live: a meter left at 3 or 6 stayed there for the rest of the grab). AI partners
  (`+0xFF0` set, offline) use `0x631110`: 10 points every 10 ticks with a per-character chance (`0x6E6C30`). (code)
- Escape: the count at 0 in phase 5 or 6 with HP 2 or more -> phase 10 (`0x563CD0`), then 11: the shove-off motion and
  zombie code 4 (`0x61040C`), the zombie's release motion (phase 6, motion 68). (code; live)
- The knife counter: `0x630E90` sets bit 7 while R1 is held (`+0xF80 & 0x440`) with an equipped weapon whose type flag
  `0x800` is set (`0x6E5940` table) and whose item count is non-zero: double rate, and at 0 phase 20 instead: zombie code
  5, its counter motion and -500 HP at its frame 102/138 (`0x66B0E0`). (code; not seen live: no knife savestate)

### The end

- Bites: the zombie (sub-state 1, `0x66B200`; phases through `0x6E54A0`) bites `+0xE` = 4 times (motions 100/101, about
  1.2 s each) after the first bite at the grab (Kevin -450, then -100 each); when they run out it writes 99 to Kevin's
  `+0xB1E` (`0x66B61C`), and Kevin's phase 6 goes to 10 (the same shove-off) (`0x610300`). No input: **8.3 s in
  stance 5** (live, twice). Down: HP under 2 -> phase 30.
- A hit on the holding zombie: its update calls `0x678750` when its hit counter `+0x13F` is set. Holding (state 2,
  sub-states 0/1 before phase 6, sub 2 before 4, sub 8 before 5): code 6 to the victim and itself (`0x678988`) - both let
  go - then the damage (`0x60F0C0`) and its hurt state (`0x6795C0`) or death. So a partner's hit frees you. (code)
- **A hit during the lunge interrupts it:** for other attack sub-states (6 among them) `0x678750` goes straight to
  `0x6795C0` (state 3, hurt) or death. Any hit the game applies counts, whatever its reaction class. The VR melee
  (cave D) and gun ray (cave I) are the game's own hits, so they already do this; nothing to add. (code; not checked
  live: no scripted hit in the 0.25 s window)

## 2. What `[VR] VrGrab` does (`pcsx2/VR/VrGrab.{h,cpp}`)

- **The cue (read only):** each vsync the enemy table is read. A zombie (`+0x14` = `0x664290`) entering state 2
  sub-state 6 with `+0x24` = the local record, within 300 units, buzzes both hands once (0.7 x HapticStrength,
  0.12 s). Log `(VR) VrGrab: enemy N lunges to grab you (d units away)`. Runs wherever first person may (private co-op too).
- **The target:** while grabbed, the zombie at the local `+0xB24` has its hurt spheres (`+0x1E0` count, `+0x1D8` shapes,
  radius `+0x0C`; `+0x1DC` centres) mapped into the XR space with `CameraDriver::VrFromGame`. The highest is the head; the
  lowest of three (the hips) is dropped.
- **The actions** (input thread, `GrabBreakout`'s poll), one per hand per 0.4 s:
  - shove: a hand within 20 cm of the head or chest sphere moving at 1.4 m/s or more toward its centre (within 60 deg);
  - hard shove (a strike to the head too): the same at 2.4 m/s and up - decisive;
  - shot / headshot (fixed 2026-10-30, branch `grabshotfix`): the trigger's press with the aim ray through the chest (a shot)
    or the head (a headshot, decisive) within 1.5 m is only a *candidate*, held 250 ms. It counts only if the game really
    fired in that window: the equipped gun's round count in the local record went down by 1-2 (`ShotWatch`, read each
    vsync from the item table, `feedback.weapon`; the same read as the weapon feedback, but it does not need the weapon
    raised). No gun equipped (melee, empty hands, a non-weapon) never makes a candidate; the game's shot is the right
    hand's (Circle is the right trigger). The head sphere on the aim ray at the press makes it a headshot (the grabbing
    zombie's HP was not used: it is not in the target record the detector holds). Before the fix any press with the ray
    through a sphere counted, with no gun, no ammo and no shot;
  - the shake (as before).
- **What an action does:**
  - always: the breakout's own presses (Circle and Cross in turn, the stick waggle) run for 0.5 s, as if shaking;
    the game's counter counts them (17-24 points of 30 in the runs below). A thump in that hand.
  - **offline only** (`NetMode::Offline`): the local record's meter loses 10 points; a decisive action empties it
    (bit 7 kept). Only in stance 5, sub-states 1-2, phases 4-6, HP 2 and up (the phases the game counts in). The
    game's grab handler then runs its own escape. Nothing else is written: not the zombie, a position, HP or a partner.
- **Online:** the cue and the presses (your own pad input) run in private co-op; the meter write is offline only.
  Partners' grabs: untouched (only the local record and the zombie holding it are read).
- No code caves (none needed: the meter is a field, the escape is the game's).
